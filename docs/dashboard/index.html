What the data says

F-14 ran at 13.0% NPT and was the most efficient producer, at 0.015 NPT hours per thousand barrels.

F-12 ran at 17.9% NPT and produced the most oil, 28.8 million barrels.

F-11 lost 56.2% of its hours to NPT and still produced 7.2 million barrels.

F-5 had 10.1% NPT but only 0.24 million barrels, the worst hours-per-barrel ratio (0.91).

Conclusion

Among the six wells with oil data, high NPT did not go with low production. The best and the largest producers had moderate NPT, and the highest-NPT well still produced. This is a small sample, so treat it as a pattern, not a proof. Water injectors are excluded.

NPT % = NPT hours / total logged hours. Data: Equinor Volve open dataset (Equinor ASA and the former Volve licence partners), shown for educational, non-commercial portfolio use; this page carries only aggregated figures. Source and full report: github.com/utkarshraj94/volve-npt-analysis.
